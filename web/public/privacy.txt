Red Queen: privacy and data notes

This site is a hackathon project (HASH-IT-OUT 2026, BITS Pilani Dubai). It runs on Base Sepolia, a public test
network. Everything on it uses test ETH, which has no value. Never send real money to any address shown here.

Wallets
- The site asks your wallet only to connect and to send ordinary transactions you approve to the project's
  contracts. It never asks for approvals, permits or signatures over your tokens.
- Everything you do on chain is public and permanent, like any blockchain transaction.

Talking to the queen
- What you type, or say when you hold to speak, is sent to the queen's server and to Google's Gemini to be
  understood and answered. Google's terms for its free API allow prompts to be used to improve its products.
- Her server keeps a short conversation memory and any recording in memory only, for the length of your visit,
  and never writes them to disk. Nothing is saved on this site.
- Her voice is synthetic.

Test ETH for judges
- From 24 November 2026, a wallet that connects with almost nothing may be sent a small amount of test ETH from the
  queen's own test wallet, so it can try an attack. It has no value.
